Dune Runner — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Dune Runner does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the game does

The game runs entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Your graphics setting is remembered on your device only. It is never sent anywhere.

Google Play version

The Android app from Google Play does not access the internet at all. Updates come through Google Play.

Other versions

The Android and iPhone versions downloaded from GitHub, and this website, check github.com for newer versions of the game. That check only downloads a small version file. No personal information is sent. GitHub and Cloudflare, which host the downloads and this website, may keep standard server logs under their own privacy policies.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
